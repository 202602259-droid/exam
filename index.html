Guide to Comparing Domestic Franchises

A simple comparison of three Korean fried-chicken franchise brands using one shared measure: the number of franchise stores reported for the 2022 base year.

한국 국내 프랜차이즈 비교 가이드입니다. 동일한 기준인 2022년 말 가맹점 수로 국내 치킨 브랜드 세 곳을 비교합니다.

1. Brands and comparison criteria

BBQ

A Korean fried-chicken franchise known for its fried chicken menu, including Golden Olive.

황금올리브 등 후라이드 치킨 메뉴로 알려진 국내 치킨 프랜차이즈입니다.

2,111 stores

Reported base year: 2022

bhc Chicken

A Korean chicken franchise with menu items such as Bburinkle and other seasoned chicken options.

뿌링클 등 다양한 시즈닝 치킨 메뉴로 알려진 국내 치킨 프랜차이즈입니다.

1,991 stores

Reported base year: 2022

Kyochon Chicken

A Korean chicken franchise known for soy-garlic, honey, and red-seasoned chicken.

간장, 허니, 레드 시리즈 치킨으로 알려진 국내 치킨 프랜차이즈입니다.

1,365 stores

Reported base year: 2022

Method / 기준: Store count (number of franchise stores), base year 2022. These figures are reported as year-end franchise-store counts based on franchise disclosure information. They are not corporate revenue or average franchisee revenue. The brand figures were reported by news coverage of the disclosure data; the official Fair Trade Commission release explains that its 2024 franchise-status report uses 2022 year-end store-count data. Check the original disclosure records before using the numbers for business decisions.
